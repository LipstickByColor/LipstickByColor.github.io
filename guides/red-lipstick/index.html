How common is red?

About 1 in 6 lipsticks is red

Red is lipstick's most iconic color, but not its most common. Pinks and nudes each outnumber it nearly two to one. Still, some brands are synonymous with red: we've counted 95 reds at Dior alone.

So what makes a red red?

Picture every lipstick color laid out on a map. Gray sits in the center. A shade has to pass three tests to be red:

  1. 1The right direction. Between pink and orange. That slice belongs to red.
  2. 2Strong color. The further from the gray center, the stronger the color. Too close and red goes soft: nude or brown on the orange side, mauve or berry on the pink side. Lean orange and it turns brick.
  3. 3Deep, not light. Lighten the same slice and you get coral and pink.

Which red are you?

We can split red into nine kinds. Two things set them apart:

Undertone Blue-red (cool)→True red→Orange-red (warm)

Plot twist

MAC Ruby Woo is famous as a blue-red. On the map it lands right in the middle, a true red. The coolest reds of the bunch? Revlon Cherries in the Snow, MAC Relentlessly Red and Maybelline Ruler.

Two Cherries in the Snow

The original Revlon crème (440) is a cool cherry red. The matte (023) borrows the name but not the color: it measures as a vivid raspberry, pinker than anything on the red map, and wears as a deep berry.

2Format

The pricier the red, the more likely it's a bullet.Liquids, with their plastic tube and wand, are cheaper to mass produce.

Most liquids dry matte.Only 56% of bullets are matte, but 76% of liquids are. Satin liquids exist too, from brands like Revlon and Rimmel.

That makes some dupes harder to find.Luxury reds lean satin bullet, affordable reds lean matte liquid. A liquid may match the color but not the look.

3Coverage and skin tone

Sheer reds change on you. Opaque reds don't.Sheer formulas let your lip color show through. For a red that matches the tube, look for “full coverage,” “opaque” or “highly pigmented.”

Swatches can look different on your lips.Lighting and editing shift a color swatch. Look for photos from people with similar skin tone and lip color.

5Packaging and extras

A pretty case doesn't change the red.Heavy metal cases, magnets, a famous logo. Nice to own, but you wear the color inside.

Going luxury? A refillable case can pay off.Buy the case once and refills after. A refill usually costs at least 20% less than buying new.
