Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't

Lipstick Color Finder has no accounts, no sign-up, and no ads. We never ask for your name or email. This page explains the little that does happen when you use the site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which parts of the site get used, so we know what to improve. When it is on, it records the pages you open and what you do in the finder and the guides: the colors you pick, the filters you apply, the brands and shades you look up or save, and searches that return nothing. It sets cookies in your browser so that repeat visits count as one visitor. Google also receives your IP address and basic device details.

If your device is set to a European time zone, analytics stays off until you allow it. Elsewhere it is on by default. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics stays off. You can change your choice here at any time:

Analytics needs JavaScript, so it is off.

What stays on your device

  • Your photos. When you match a color from a photo, the image is read by your browser. It is never uploaded or stored.
  • Your favorites and preferences. Saved shades, your finish and tone preferences, and your analytics choice are kept in your browser's storage. We can't see them, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Shared lists. A link you create to share your favorites carries the list inside the link itself. Anyone with the link can see that list.

Services the site relies on

Loading the site means your browser contacts a few other services, which see your IP address as any website does:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Supabase serves the lipstick catalogue, and the search in the guides.
  • Google Fonts, unpkg and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries.
  • Brand and retailer websites serve the product photos shown in results.
