Find lipstick by color

Search nearly 17,000 lipsticks, liquid lipsticks, glosses, and lip products by their actual color, not brand shade names. Pick a shade on the color wheel, find dupes for a lipstick you already love, match a color from a photo, or enter a hex code. Filter by finish, brand, format, and price.

Every shade is measured from product images with a machine learning pipeline and compared by perceptual color distance (ΔE in CIELAB), so matches look alike to the eye.
